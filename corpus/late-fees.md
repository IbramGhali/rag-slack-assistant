# Late Fees

## Late fee amount

A late fee of $25 is charged when an instalment is more than 5 days overdue.

## Limits

No more than 3 late fees are charged on a single payment plan.

## Hardship waiver

Students experiencing financial hardship can ask Student Accounts to waive late fees. Waivers are decided within 5 business days.
