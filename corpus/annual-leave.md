# Annual Leave

## Entitlement

Full-time staff receive 22 days of annual leave per year. Part-time staff receive a pro rata entitlement.

## Requests

Leave requests must be submitted at least 10 business days in advance through the HR system and approved by the staff member's manager.

## Carry over

Up to 5 days of unused annual leave can be carried over to the next year.
