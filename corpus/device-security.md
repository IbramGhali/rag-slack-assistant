# Device Security

## Encryption

All company laptops use full-disk encryption.

## Screen lock

Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.

## Lost devices

A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT service desk within 1 hour so it can be wiped remotely.
