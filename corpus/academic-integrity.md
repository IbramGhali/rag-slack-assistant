# Academic Integrity

## Generative AI

Generative AI tools can be used in an assessment only when the assessment instructions say so, and their use must be acknowledged.

## Breaches

A first minor breach results in a formal warning and a resubmission. Serious or repeated breaches are referred to the academic integrity panel.
