# Password and Login Standard

## Password length

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is required for email, the student system and remote access.

## Reuse and rotation

The last 10 passwords cannot be reused. Passwords are changed only when a compromise is suspected, not on a fixed schedule.
