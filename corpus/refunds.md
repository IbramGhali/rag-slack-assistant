# Refund Policy

## Refund window

Students may request a full refund within 14 days of enrolment, provided they have completed less than 10 percent of the course.

## Processing time

Approved refunds are paid within 10 business days to the original payment method.

## Partial refunds

After the refund window, refunds are calculated pro rata for units the student has not started.

## Exceptions

Students with a documented medical condition or bereavement can apply for a refund outside the window. Supporting documents must be supplied within 30 days.
