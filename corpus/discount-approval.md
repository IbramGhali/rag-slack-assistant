# Sales Discount Approval

## Agent limit

Sales agents can offer a discount of up to 15 percent.

## Higher discounts

Discounts above 15 percent need team lead approval, up to a maximum of 25 percent.

## Combining offers

Discounts cannot be combined with scholarships.
