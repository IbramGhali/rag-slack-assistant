# Scholarships

## Merit scholarship

Merit scholarships reduce the course fee by up to 20 percent.

## How to apply

Applications close 30 days before the course start date and must include a personal statement of no more than 500 words.

## Limits

A student can hold only one scholarship at a time, and scholarships cannot be combined with sales discounts.
