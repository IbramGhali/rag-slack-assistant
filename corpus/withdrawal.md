# Withdrawing From a Course

## How to withdraw

Students withdraw through the student portal. Withdrawal is effective from the date the request is submitted.

## Census date

Withdrawals after the census date, which is 28 days after the course start, are not eligible for a refund.

## Academic record

A withdrawn unit appears on the academic record with a grade of W and does not affect the grade average.
