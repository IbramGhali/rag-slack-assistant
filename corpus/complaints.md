# Student Complaints

## Lodging a complaint

Students can lodge a complaint within 30 days of the issue through the student portal.

## Timeframes

Complaints are acknowledged within 2 business days and resolved within 20 business days.

## Escalation

If the student is not satisfied, they can escalate to the independent education ombudsman.
