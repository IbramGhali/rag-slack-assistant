# Payment Plans

## Eligibility

Any student enrolling in a course priced over $1,500 can apply for a payment plan. Applications are assessed at enrolment and approved within two business days.

## Instalment options

Plans run for 6, 12 or 18 monthly instalments. The first instalment is due on the course start date, and later instalments are due on the same day each month.

## Missed payments

If an instalment is missed, the student receives a reminder after 3 days. Course access is paused after 21 days without payment and restored within one business day of the overdue amount being paid.

## Changing a plan

A student can change the plan length once per course by contacting Student Accounts. Changes take effect from the next instalment date.
